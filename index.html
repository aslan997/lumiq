Got questions?

Not at all. Lumiq is built for business users and analysts alike. You can ask questions in plain English, use drag-and-drop to build dashboards, and connect data sources without writing a single line of code. SQL access is available for power users who want it.

The free Starter plan gives you up to 3 seats, 3 data source connections, and 5 dashboards with 7 days of data history—forever free, no credit card required. When you sign up, you also automatically start a 14-day trial of Pro features so you can explore everything before committing.

Yes. Lumiq is SOC 2 Type II certified. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We support role-based access control, audit logs, SSO/SAML on Enterprise, and data residency options for EU and APAC regions. We never sell or share your data with third parties.

We offer dedicated migration support for teams coming from Tableau, Looker, Power BI, or Metabase. Our onboarding team will help you map your existing dashboards and data models to Lumiq. Enterprise customers get a white-glove migration service included at no extra cost.
